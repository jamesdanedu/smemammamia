Where you sit

Every ticket has its own seat, so there is no need to arrive early to get a good spot. Row A is nearest the stage and the letters run back from there to row — there is no row I or O, so nobody mistakes them for 1 and 0. Seat 1 is on your left as you face the stage and the numbers run straight across to on your right, with the centre aisle between . A cross aisle runs between rows .

Accessible seating

The seats marked in purple are kept for anyone who needs step-free access or a wheelchair space. They sit beside the cross aisle and the side gangways, so nobody has to squeeze past. A wheelchair space takes the place of two seats with the chairs taken away, and a companion sits alongside. These seats are not sold online — use Need accessible seating? on the booking page and the team will be in touch to arrange them with you.
