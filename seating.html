Where you sit

Every ticket has a seat number on it, so there is no need to arrive early to get a good spot. Row A is nearest the stage and the letters run back from there. Seat 1 is on your left as you face the stage, and the numbers run straight across all three blocks to 28 on your right — the row letter is on the end chair of every row, and stewards will be on each aisle.

The four seats marked on the plan are wheelchair spaces: the chair is taken away so the wheelchair stands in its place, right off the middle gangway with a companion seat beside it. Ask for one when you book — tick the access box at step five — and we will keep it for you.

Setting out

Measurements for whoever puts the chairs out. Every distance is from the wall the stage is built against, to the front legs of that row.
