About the show

Mamma Mia!

The Musical

A daughter's search for her father, told on a Greek island in the songs of ABBA.

The Story

Sophie Sheridan is twenty years old, getting married tomorrow, and has never known who her father is. Going through her mother's things, she finds an old diary from the summer before she was born — and three names in it.

So she does something she cannot take back. She writes to all three, signs her mother's name, and invites them to the wedding. Sam, Bill and Harry arrive on the island expecting a reunion with the woman they each remember, none of them knowing about the other two.

Donna — who runs the taverna, who has raised Sophie alone, and who has spent twenty years not talking about that summer — has no idea any of them are coming. Her old bandmates Tanya and Rosie do arrive as planned, ready to be Donna and the Dynamos one more time, and walk straight into the middle of it.

"On the eve of her wedding, a daughter's quest to discover the identity of her father brings three men from her mother's past back to the island they last visited twenty years ago."

It's funny, it's warm, it turns unexpectedly moving somewhere around the second act — and the songs are the ones the whole room already knows.

Who's Who

Thirteen named roles, plus a full company of islanders and wedding guests

Sophie Sheridan
Born and raised on the island, marrying Sky tomorrow, and determined to find out which of three men is her father.
Donna Sheridan
Sophie's mother. Runs the taverna, holds the whole island together, and has said nothing about that summer for twenty years.
Sam Carmichael
An architect who left the island and never quite left it behind. One of the three.
Bill Austin
A travel writer and sailor who has kept moving ever since. One of the three.
Harry Bright
A very respectable banker who was once, briefly and gloriously, Head Banger Harry. One of the three.
Sky
Sophie's fiancé. Patient, in love, and slowly realising the wedding has a subplot.
Tanya
Donna's old bandmate. Wealthy, three times divorced, and enjoying every minute of it.
Rosie
The other Dynamo. Warm, very funny, and entirely without a filter.
Lisa
Sophie's friend and bridesmaid, in on the secret from the start.
Ali
Sophie's other bridesmaid, and the one asking the awkward questions.
Pepper
Works the taverna bar. Utterly undeterred by Tanya's lack of interest.
Eddie
Sky's friend, running the water sports and the stag night.
Father Alexandrios
The island priest, booked to conduct a wedding that keeps changing shape.
Ensemble
Islanders, guests and dancers — and the reason the taverna scenes fill the hall.
